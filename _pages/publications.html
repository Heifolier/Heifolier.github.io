LLM-Guided Hierarchical Belief Planning for Online Semantic Exploration and Task Execution

Shuo Zhang, Zhongkui Li, Meng Guo, and Yanran Wei

IEEE CAC 2026 · Published · First author

We propose a hierarchical belief-space planning framework that combines LLM-generated object-location priors with online semantic exploration. Against the SOTA baseline, the method reduces average execution cost by 18.6% and travel distance by 16.9%, while also decreasing the number of LLM queries.

Melding LLM and Temporal Logic for Reliable Human-Swarm Collaboration in Complex Environments

Junfeng Chen, Yuxiao Zhu, An Zhuo, Xintong Zhang, Shuo Zhang, Guanghui Wen, Xiwang Dong, Meng Guo, and Zhongkui Li

Submitted to Science Robotics

We develop a neuro-symbolic human-swarm framework that integrates LLM-based task generation, temporal-logic constraints, uncertainty awareness, and real-time event monitoring. In large-scale experiments involving more than 40 robots, 41 tasks, and 155 subtasks, the framework improves task success by 260%, increases completed tasks by 132%, and reduces operator intervention by 77%.

SharpSurg: Foundation-Model-Based Autonomous Long-Horizon Robotic Surgery in Vivo

Submitted to Nature

A foundation-model-based system for autonomous long-horizon robotic surgery. My work focused on respiratory-phase-conditioned VLA motion generation, multimodal training, and deployment on the Toumai multi-arm surgical robot.

Formal Logic-based Cooperative Task Planning for Multi-robot Systems: Survey of Recent Advances and Future Directions

Shuo Zhang et al.

Acta Automatica Sinica · Published · Student third author

This survey reviews formal-logic-based cooperative planning for multi-robot systems, including natural-language task understanding, translation into PDDL/LTL/STL specifications, task decomposition, and plan generation. It also discusses limitations in hallucination control, constraint completeness, and interpretability.
